## Selection Criteria Quick Reference

### ✅ Accept if:
- Transcript available or can be obtained (auto-generated acceptable)
- Shows concrete first message examples
- Author has practical experience (practitioner, not just theorist)
- Covers cold email OR cold DM strategies
- Discusses response rates, real conversations, or message breakdowns

### ❌ Reject if:
- No transcript available or obtainable
- Generic tips without actual message examples
- Compilation without analysis or author
- Selling a course with only teasers (mark conflict of interest if included)
- Mass email / spam techniques
- No practical experience demonstrated
